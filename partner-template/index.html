Your data

This starter shows PhilSA's layers only. To make it your agency's map, add your own GeoJSON (farms, assets, claims…) — set MY_DATA in the source and it's drawn on top of the satellite layers.

How this works

PhilSA hosts the Earth-observation layers; this page just points at their open STAC + tile endpoints (see the PHILSA config at the top of the source). No PhilSA library or key. Full recipes: INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md.
